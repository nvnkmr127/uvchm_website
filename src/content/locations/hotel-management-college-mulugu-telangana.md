## **Our Industry-Aligned Hotel Management Programs**

We offer a suite of specialized diploma programs designed to fit your career goals and educational background. Whether you've just completed your 10th, 12th, or are a graduate, we have a course that’s perfect for you. Each program combines intensive classroom learning with mandatory, paid industrial training at premier properties.

### **[Diploma in Hotel Management](https://uvchm.com/diploma-course-in-hotel-management/) (6 Months Study + 6 Months Training)**

*   **Ideal For:** Students who have completed their 10th or 12th and want a quick, intensive entry into the hospitality industry.
*   **What You'll Learn:** This foundational program covers the four core pillars of hotel operations: Food Production (Culinary), F&B Service, Front Office Management, and Accommodation Operations (Housekeeping).
*   **Career Path:** Perfect for securing entry-level positions in top hotels and quick-service restaurants, with a strong foundation for future growth.

### **[Advance Diploma in Hotel Management](https://uvchm.com/advance-diploma-course-in-hotel-management/) (12 Months Study + 6 Months Training)**

*   **Ideal For:** 12th pass or graduate students seeking a deeper, more comprehensive understanding of hotel operations and management principles.
*   **What You'll Learn:** Builds upon the core diploma subjects with additional modules in Hospitality Sales & Marketing, Basic Accountancy, and Event Management. This course prepares you for supervisory roles.
*   **Career Path:** Opens doors to roles like Team Leader, Shift Supervisor, and specialized positions in hotel sales or event coordination.

### **Master Diploma in Hotel Management (18 Months Study + 6 Months Training)**

*   **Ideal For:** Ambitious students who aim for managerial positions early in their careers. This is our most exhaustive undergraduate program.
*   **What You'll Learn:** An extensive curriculum covering all aspects of the Advance Diploma, plus advanced subjects like Human Resource Management in Hospitality, Facility Management, and Strategic Management.
*   **Career Path:** This program is your fast track to positions like Assistant Manager, Department Head, and other leadership roles within the hospitality sector.

### **[PG Diploma in Hotel Management](https://uvchm.com/pg-diploma-in-hotel-management/) (12 Months Study + 6 Months Training)**

*   **Ideal For:** Graduates from any stream (BA, B.Com, B.Sc, etc.) who wish to pivot and build a high-flying career in the hotel industry.
*   **What You'll Learn:** A postgraduate-level intensive program focusing on the strategic and managerial aspects of running a hotel. It covers financial management, revenue optimization, luxury brand management, and advanced guest relations.
*   **Career Path:** Designed for direct entry into management trainee programs at 5-star hotel chains and corporate hospitality roles.
