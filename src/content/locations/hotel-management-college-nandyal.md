### **Master Diploma in Hotel Management (18 Months Study + 6 Months Training)**

Our most extensive undergraduate program, designed to create future leaders in the hospitality industry. This course dives deep into the strategic and managerial aspects of running a successful hotel.

*   **Eligibility:** SSC (10th) Pass or Fail, Intermediate (12th) Pass or Fail.

*   **Ideal For:** Highly ambitious individuals targeting managerial positions like Duty Manager, Department Head, or even future General Managers.
